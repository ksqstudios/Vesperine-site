Vesperine An evening journal

Vesperine 2.1

Two pages on iPhone Duo, and nights you can delete.

Built for iPhone Duo

Unfold iPhone Duo in landscape and the evening page opens into a spread. Tonight's page sits on the left, where you write. Your recent nights sit open beside it on the right, so you can read what came before without leaving the page you are on. Folded, or held upright, Vesperine is the single quiet page it has always been.

Delete a night

Any entry can now be deleted, from Tonight or from the Archive. It is removed from this device and from your iCloud, and Looking back will never return a line from it.

A steadier Mirror

Reflections now rest on words you actually wrote, and are less likely to repeat an entry back to you.
